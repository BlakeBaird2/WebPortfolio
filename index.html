About Me

I'm an Information Systems undergraduate at BYU with a deep passion for technology that extends far beyond the classroom. Whether I'm building websites, gaming with friends, or developing innovative solutions, I thrive in the digital world.

My journey in tech has taken me from implementing AI-powered automation systems during my internship at River Logistics, where I streamlined client communications using cutting-edge TMS software, to competing in the BYU Marriott Case Competition for Adminify AI, where my team placed 2nd out of 98 teams. These experiences have fueled my ambition to create an international tech consulting firm that helps organizations optimize their systems through collaborative, tech-enabled solutions.

When I'm not coding or optimizing workflows, you'll find me outdoors with friends. I'm passionate about water sports, from my time on dive team, swim team, and water polo, to cliff jumping adventures, wake surfing, and catching waves in the ocean. There's nothing quite like relaxing on the beach after a day in the water. This balance between technology and the outdoors keeps me energized and creative.

I love collaborating with teams to build impactful projects and bring ideas to life through code. From full-stack development to workflow automation, I'm driven by the challenge of turning complex problems into elegant, efficient solutions.
